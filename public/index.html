Watch together.
Same moment, every screen.

Create a room, share the link, and play a YouTube, Facebook, Vimeo or Twitch video, or upload your own file. Everyone in the room stays on the same second.

  • Links play from the source. YouTube and Facebook videos play straight from those sites, so nothing gets stored.
  • Uploads stream from the server. Your file is hosted here and streamed to every viewer.
  • The server keeps time. Anyone who drifts or joins late is moved to the right spot automatically.
